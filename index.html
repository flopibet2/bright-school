About Us - Bright Star School Chak No 493 JB

Welcome to Bright Star School Chak No 493 JB, located in Tehsil Shorkot, District Jhang (EMIS: 33230099). Our institution is dedicated to providing quality education, empowering young girls with knowledge, moral values, and essential academic skills to help them excel in their futures.

Under the visionary leadership of Principal Dr. Noor, our school maintains a disciplined, safe, and progressive environment. We focus on academic excellence, curricular activities, character building, and modern digital transparency through our official school web portal.

Privacy Policy

At Bright Star School Chak No 493 JB School Portal, accessible from our official platform, we prioritize the privacy and security of our students, staff, and visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by us and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We collect official school monitoring data, staff attendance records, academic performance metrics, and top position holder details strictly for administrative transparency and educational updates. No personal or sensitive student data is sold or shared with third parties.
