About Us - Bright Stars School System Chak No 493 JB

Welcome to Bright Stars School System Chak No 493 JB, located in Tehsil Shorkot, District Jhang (EMIS: 332316158). Our institution is dedicated to providing quality education, empowering young girls with knowledge, moral values, and essential academic skills to help them excel in their futures.

Under the visionary leadership of Principal Ayesha Noor, our school maintains a disciplined, safe, and progressive environment. We focus on academic excellence, curricular activities, character building, and modern digital transparency through our official school web portal.
